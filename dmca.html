DMCA & Copyright Policy

Information for copyright owners and representatives who wish to report potentially infringing material displayed on StreamESPN.

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

1. Copyright and Intellectual Property

StreamESPN is an independent sports information website featuring fixtures, scores, schedules, team information, and match details. Some sports data, team logos, tournament emblems, and images may be supplied through third-party APIs or external providers. Ownership of third-party trademarks and copyrighted materials remains with the respective rights holders.

Access to an image or other asset through an API does not, by itself, establish permission for every use. We aim to respect applicable intellectual property rights and review credible reports of unauthorized content.

2. Reporting Copyright Concerns

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized representative and believe that specific material on StreamESPN infringes your rights, please submit a written complaint through our Contact Us page. Include enough detail for us to identify and investigate the material.

3. What to Include in a Copyright Notice

  1. Your physical or electronic signature as the copyright owner or authorized agent.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. The exact URL(s) of the allegedly infringing material on StreamESPN, with a description that helps locate it.
  4. Your name and appropriate contact details, including an email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the rights owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

4. Review and Response

We review sufficiently detailed reports and may remove content, disable access to material or links, contact relevant providers, or seek clarification where appropriate. We may preserve information relating to a complaint as reasonably necessary to address it or meet legal obligations.

Submitting a report does not guarantee removal. A complaint must identify a legitimate rights concern, and applicable exceptions or permissions may be relevant.

5. Third-Party APIs, Images, and Logos

Some sports images, logos, and statistics are furnished by third-party providers. If a rights concern involves an asset supplied by an external API, please provide the specific page URL and asset details. We can review the displayed material and, where appropriate, contact the supplier or disable its display.

6. External Websites and Links

Some pages contain third-party advertising, affiliate, and referral links. External sites are independently operated; StreamESPN cannot remove content hosted on sites it does not control. We may, however, review and remove links displayed on StreamESPN when warranted.

7. Counter-Notifications and Disputes

If content is removed following a copyright complaint and you believe the removal occurred due to mistake or misidentification, contact us with the relevant URL, a description of the removed content, your explanation, and suitable contact information. Where formal counter-notification procedures apply, additional legally required statements and details may be needed.

8. False or Misleading Reports

Please submit complaints only when you have a reasonable basis to assert the rights involved. Knowingly material misrepresentations in copyright notices or counter-notices may have legal consequences.

9. Notice Contact

Copyright questions and reports can be initiated through https://streamespn.org/contact-us.

Important: This page provides a reporting channel but does not itself represent that StreamESPN has registered a designated DMCA agent with the U.S. Copyright Office. If a formal designated-agent process is applicable, the relevant agent details and registration must be separately completed.
